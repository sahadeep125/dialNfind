# Kaajva SEO strategy: getting found when Siliguri searches for a local pro

**Goal:** when someone in Siliguri types "mixer grinder repair near me", "electrician in Siliguri" or
"AC repair Pradhan Nagar", a Kaajva page is on the first screen of Google, it shows nearby professionals
straight away, and the visitor calls or WhatsApps one of them.

## 1. How the research was done (and its limits)

No paid keyword tool (Ahrefs, Semrush) was available, so **volumes and difficulty below are relative
estimates, not counts**. They are based on:

- What ranks today for Siliguri service searches. For example, "mixer grinder repair Siliguri" returns
  national brand blogs (Crompton, Wonderchef), a Kolkata Urban Company page, and thin directories that
  list *products* rather than repairers (dial4trade, IndiaMART). Nobody serves the actual intent: a list
  of repairers near me with phone numbers. That gap repeats across appliance and repair searches.
- Urban Company builds city + locality pages for Kolkata ("AC service repair Belghoria") but **not for
  Siliguri**. Mannubhai has a single `/siliguri/ac-service` page. Justdial and Sulekha have Siliguri
  pages but they are lead-capture forms, slow, and full of ads.
- Google autocomplete patterns: "<service> near me", "<service> in siliguri", "<service> siliguri",
  "<appliance> repair near me", "<appliance> not working".
- India-wide trends: "near me" searches keep growing, appliance searches are increasingly typed in
  Hindi and Bengali, and AI Overviews / Maps answers now sit above classic results.

**Before writing new pages, confirm numbers** in Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account, set
location to Siliguri / Darjeeling district) and, after a month of traffic, in Search Console
(Performance > Queries).

## 4. URL structure

Short, lowercase, hyphenated, one meaning per URL, and stable (never change a live slug without a 301):

```
/                                         Home: "local services & repair near you in Siliguri"
/services                                 All services
/services/{category}                      Electricians in Siliguri
/services/{category}/{service}            Mixer grinder repair in Siliguri
/services/{category}/in/{locality}        Electricians in Pradhan Nagar, Siliguri (only with 3+ pros)
/providers/{business-name-locality}       One business or professional
/guides/{topic}                           Cost and how-to answers
/search?q=…                               Live search (noindex; filters create endless URLs)
```

Next step (P2): `/services/{category}/{service}/in/{locality}` for the highest-volume pairs
(AC repair, mobile repair, electrician, beauty parlour) once a locality has 3+ pros for that service, so
thin pages are never created.

| Page | Title (≤ 60 chars) | Meta description (150–160) | H1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service | `Mixer Grinder Repair in Siliguri: Near You` | Find mixer grinder repair technicians near you in Siliguri. Motor, jar, blade and switch repairs. Compare distance and hours, then call directly. | Mixer grinder repair near you in Siliguri |
| Service + locality | `Electricians in Pradhan Nagar, Siliguri` | {N} electricians in and around Pradhan Nagar… | Electricians in Pradhan Nagar, Siliguri |
| Provider | `{Name}: {Service} in {Locality}, Siliguri` | {Rating if any}. {About}. Call {Name} directly on Kaajva. | {Name} |
| Guide | `{Question}: Prices in Siliguri (2026)` | Direct answer in one line… | {Question} |

Rules: service first, then the place; "near me" only where it reads naturally; no keyword stuffing;
one H1; H2s are real questions or the obvious next step ("Mixer grinder repair shops near you",
"What does it cost?", "Questions people ask").

## 6. On-page content for service pages

1. H1 + one line that answers the search ("12 mixer grinder repair technicians near Sevoke Road").
2. The list of pros, nearest first, with Call and WhatsApp (the reason the visitor came).
3. "What does it cost in Siliguri?" honest ranges.
4. Common problems and what fixes them (matches problem searches).
5. FAQ (FAQPage schema).
6. Related services and nearby localities (internal links).

## 7. Provider profile pages

Every live listing is a page that can rank for the business's own name and for "{service} {locality}".

- Title and description come from the API (`server/src/services/seo.ts`) and name the service and area.
- Visible NAP: name, full address with locality and pincode, phone (behind the Call button), hours.
- Services offered link to their service pages (internal links that pass relevance both ways).
- "Similar pros nearby" links to 6 neighbouring profiles, so crawlers find every listing in 2–3 hops.
- No empty sections or placeholders: Google treats near-empty templates as thin content.
- JSON-LD: a LocalBusiness subtype (`Electrician`, `Plumber`, `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`…) with
  `geo`, `address`, `telephone`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `areaServed`, `aggregateRating` only when
  real reviews exist, and `BreadcrumbList`.

## 9. Internal linking

- Header and footer link to the top services. Home links popular **service pages**, not `/search`.
- Home "Popular searches in Siliguri" block links service + locality pages.
- Service pages link related services, nearby localities and guides; guides link back to services.
- Profiles link their services, their category, and similar pros.

## 10. Technical (in place)

Canonical URLs, sitemap index with every service, area and live profile, `robots.txt` blocking search
and the dashboard, `noindex` on private pages, generated OG images, cached pages for fast TTFB, Core Web
Vitals friendly images (`next/image`), verified crawlers allowed past rate limits.

## 11. Off-site (owner)

1. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools, submit `https://kaajva.in/sitemap.xml`.
2. Google Business Profile for Kaajva (Ghogomali office), consistent NAP everywhere.
3. Ask listed businesses to link "Find us on Kaajva" from their own sites and WhatsApp catalogues.
4. Local citations: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Facebook page, Apple Business Connect.
5. Local press and community groups (Siliguri Facebook groups, Uttarbanga Sambad).
6. Promote the iOS app (App Store listing) and add the Play Store link when the Android app ships.

## 12. Measure

Monthly in Search Console: queries with high impressions and position 8–20 are the cheapest wins;
improve those pages' intro and FAQ. Track calls and WhatsApp clicks per landing page in GA4
(`contact_clicked`) to see which pages bring customers, not just visits.
